What Pure Water Means for Exterior Cleaning

Understanding the role of RO + DI water treatment.

Why water quality matters

Water can contain dissolved minerals that leave residue as it dries. Reducing those minerals can help limit spotting after a rinse on exterior glass and other suitable surfaces.

Reverse osmosis and deionisation

Reverse osmosis uses a membrane to reduce dissolved contaminants. Deionisation uses ion-exchange resin to remove remaining dissolved ions.

The finish depends on more than water

The original deposits, surface condition, cleaning action and rinsing all affect the result. Established mineral stains or stubborn contamination may need a different treatment.

For solar installations, the proposed method also needs to follow the relevant panel manufacturer’s guidance.
