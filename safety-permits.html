BEFORE THE FLIGHT

The plan extends
to the ground.

Our operating plan considers the cleaning area and the activity around it. We coordinate the work zone, access and site arrangements with the property representative before cleaning starts.

01 / PERMISSIONS

Review the location.

Flight permissions account for the site, proposed operation and applicable conditions. An Operator Permit alone does not authorise every flight.

02 / SITE

Prepare the work area.

We plan pedestrian and vehicle controls, equipment positioning, hose routing and arrangements for surrounding property.

03 / CONDITIONS

Work within the limits.

Weather and site conditions are checked against the operating plan. Work pauses or is rescheduled when conditions are unsuitable.
