Drone cleaning for low-rise commercial properties

Altitude Robotics cleans exterior glass, façades and cladding for low-rise properties with less access setup and a digital record of the completed work.

Illustrative property image · Video to be supplied

The incumbent

Scaffold, boom lifts and manual squeegee work can cost more than the façade warrants.

For owners and FMs, low-rise cleaning still brings access equipment, blocked frontage and tenant coordination. The goal is to remove the access overhead where the site suits drone cleaning.

The Altitude approach

Clean the elevation without building a large access setup around it.

  • Less mobilisation

    No scaffold or elevated platform is required for suitable drone-cleaned areas.

  • Keep the property moving

    Cleaning is phased around entrances, customers, tenants and deliveries.

  • Document the result

    Each job can hand back before / after imagery, coverage and condition notes.

Typical properties

SCOPE

What is included

  • Surfaces: exterior glazing, compatible façades, awnings, podium walls and cladding.
  • Soiling: dust, traffic film, bird residue and light organic buildup.
  • Included: site plan, cleaning zones, surface-appropriate rinse and visual QA.
OPERATING LIMITS

What we will not force

  • Narrow setbacks, trees, wires, signage and public-road interfaces can make a façade unsuitable for drone access.
  • Public areas need a controlled exclusion zone; we do not operate through uncontrolled pedestrian traffic.
  • Unsafe weather or surfaces incompatible with water / spray pressure are excluded.
  • Standoff, pressure and usable reach are confirmed after site survey rather than assumed from building height.

Common questions

Will the property need to close?

Usually not. We control the active work zone and coordinate it around entrances and operating hours.

Can you clean coated glass?

We check the coating and manufacturer guidance before choosing the method.

Is every low-rise site suitable?

No. Trees, wires, narrow setbacks, public traffic and surface condition can make conventional access the better method.
