A few common
questions.

Is drone cleaning suitable for my property?

We assess the surface material, building layout, access and type of soiling before recommending a method. Recessed areas and deposits that need scraping or close-contact treatment may require another approach.

How high can you clean?

The working height depends on the location, approved flight conditions and cleaning setup. Share the site address and approximate height so we can assess your project.

Do we need to arrange the flight permits?

We identify and coordinate the flight-permit requirements. You help confirm site details, access and building-management approvals. Work proceeds once the required permissions are in place.

Is it cheaper than traditional cleaning?

It can reduce access-equipment and setup costs on suitable sites. The final price depends on the area, surface condition, access, permissions and cleaning requirements.

Can you clean glass and solar panels?

RO + DI treatment reduces dissolved minerals and can help limit spotting after rinsing. We assess the surface and soiling first, and check manufacturer guidance for solar panels.

Will the drone record inside apartments?

Camera use should focus on flight control and agreed exterior-work documentation. We discuss privacy arrangements with building management and do not intentionally film inside homes.

What happens if the weather changes?

If wind, rain or visibility make conditions unsuitable, we pause or reschedule the work and agree the next available window with you.
